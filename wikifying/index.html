THE IDEA

Conferences are
knowledge hubs.

Professional conferences bring together experts, researchers, speakers and communities who may become important sources of knowledge for Wikimedia projects.

“Wikifying” means making that knowledge more visible, structured, reusable and openly licensed.

WHAT DOES IT MEAN?

One conference.
Many Wikimedia opportunities.

01.1 · PLANNING

Start early.
Think strategically.

  • Decide what role you want at the event.
  • Plan funding, travel and accommodation.
  • Assess the skills and time available.
  • Choose your Wikimedia focus.
  • Create an on-Wiki project page.

01.2 · PITCHING

Explain why a
Wikimedian belongs there.

“Assume that conference organisers haven't talked to a Wikimedian before.”

Make sure
people know you're there.

✓ Agree your space and equipment.
✓ Understand attendee-data permissions.
✓ Coordinate with the conference photographer.
✓ Ask organisers to announce your presence.
✓ Provide a photo and short description.

03.3 · CONFERENCE VIDEO

Don't forget
the recordings.

Encourage organisers to publish conference talks under an open licence.

One recording can become:
  • A clip for a Wikipedia article
  • An audio introduction
  • Evidence for a subject's work
  • A reusable Commons resource
